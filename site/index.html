A day at Kavanagh

The day does not need a schedule.
It just needs room.

Instead of listing amenities, follow the feeling of the place from first splash to last light.

10:00 · Morning / swim

Start where the water is.

Arrive, drop the bags, and let the pool set the pace. Kavanagh’s water-play area and open pool deck make the first part of the day easy to understand: just get in.

02

14:00 · Afternoon / unwind

Leave room for nothing.

Take a cottage, settle into the shade, eat, talk, nap, watch the water. The middle of the day is intentionally unplanned—the kind of time that makes a resort day feel longer.
